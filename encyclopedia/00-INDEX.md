# The UNI Encyclopedia

A science-honest public reference work about the UNI program: a **developmental
active-inference SIMULATION**, a bounded peek into a toy world, **never a person and
never a mind.** One engine, shown across scales.

**Honest program position, printed and never softened: ~2 of 11+ developmental rungs earned.**

Every chapter authors *against* two files and never ahead of them:

- the single source of truth, [`CLAIM-LEDGER.md`](CLAIM-LEDGER.md) (the append-only,
  evidence-classed claim ledger; four states only: PASS / FAIL / NEGATIVE / PENDING);
- the authoring blueprint, [`MASTER-PLAN.md`](MASTER-PLAN.md) (the front-matter
  constitution, the A–U rubric, the standing fences, and the per-chapter spec).

If any chapter's prose disagrees with the ledger, **the ledger wins and the prose is wrong.**
No chapter states a claim above its recorded evidence class, omits a recorded falsifier, or
hides a recorded negative. Every PASS is published *with* the negative that travels with it;
every chapter ends with a "Falsify this" close.

## The standing red lines (every chapter honors them)

Never claim AGI / human-level / "understands"; never consciousness / sentience / aware
(functional self-awareness may be described only at L8; phenomenal sentience is explicitly
DISCLAIMED); never "active inference demonstrated" (the framing lens only); never
"created life" / "measurable awareness" as a claim (an OPEN falsifiable question, never an
answer); never "beats LLMs" (World C is a COUNT baseline, ~10–15% behind backprop LLMs by a
chosen design trade); never raise a claim above its source class; the parked frontier is a
**ledger-scoped exhausted search envelope**, never "K≥3 exhausted" / "Sec-0.6(B) achieved."
No PII, no patent-level math (textbook framing only). The full list and the SIGNED
forbidden-phrasings red-line live in [`MASTER-PLAN.md`](MASTER-PLAN.md) §FM-3.
