# Mv1 - The honesty fence is the pitch

The honest position of this chapter is small and load-bearing: publishing our negatives, fencing our claims, and tracing every figure to a ledger row is a **method**, not a capability. It is a discipline we can show works (the press kit was built this way, and it holds together), and a posture we choose to lead with in public. It is not, and never becomes, evidence that the underlying science is proven. "We are honest about what we have not shown" is a true sentence and a useful one; it is not a substitute for "we proved it," and any reader who hears the second when we say the first has been misled. This chapter exists to make that confusion impossible. The whole program remains a developmental active-inference SIMULATION, a bounded peek into a toy world, never a person and never a mind, with the honest standing position printed in full: **~2 of 11+ developmental rungs earned.**

The pitch, stated plainly: we put the things we have **not** proven on the front page, we attach a falsifier to every claim, and we invite you to break the work rather than fund it. The differentiator is not a result. It is the refusal to overclaim, made operational.

## The posture, and the one thing it is

The reusable discipline is recorded as method claim **M15 (Honesty-fence-as-the-pitch)**, evidence class **method**. It has two halves. First: publish the negatives front-and-center, in a boxed "what we have NOT proven," and treat the published negatives as the credibility rather than something to bury. Second, a hard, test-enforced **vocabulary-leak guard**: public copy never externalizes the internal framework vocabulary (the active-inference / expected-free-energy / free-energy names) and never features an internal channel handle; it says "count baselines," "prediction-error," "LOOP not LEAP." The call-to-action that the method mandates is exact, and it is the spine of this whole chapter: the CTA is **"help us independently verify," not "fund the vision."**

Because M15 is carded `method`, it asserts no capability and cannot be raised into one. Its falsifier is a *violation*, not a measurement: public copy that carries the fenced framework vocabulary, that features a channel handle, that states an inflated claim, or that asks the reader to fund the vision rather than to verify the work. The fence is the claim here. Living inside it is the entire content. The discipline is to make the fence *louder* under pressure, not wider: even under a partner-facing push to put a chip on the board soon, the package moved no claim line.

## The proof that the method runs (with its travelling fence)

The method is not only asserted; it has been executed end-to-end once, and that execution is recorded as **TA3 (no-API publishing proven end-to-end, press kit)**, evidence class **E**. The seven-document press kit, plus a regenerable PDF, was authored entirely on the Claude subscription / CLI with **no autonomous server-side LLM in the authoring path**, and every figure in it traces to a verified ledger row backed by a one-command reproduction. The kit leads with the one proven thing (the count-baseline language PASS), puts a boxed "What UNI has NOT proven" on page one (active inference still NEGATIVE; roughly 10 to 15 percent behind backprop LLMs on char-perplexity by a chosen design trade; the audit remediation; the Phase J caveat), and demotes forward vision to a clearly labelled "belief, not proof" box.

The fence that must travel with TA3 is its own evidence class. **E means test-covered, not feature-working** (a Class E pass never satisfies a Class A criterion). TA3's falsifiers are concrete and they are the discipline: a press-kit figure that cannot be traced to a ledger row, a one-command reproduction that fails, or a server-side content LLM discovered in the authoring path. The claim is precisely "the no-API model demonstrably produced this artifact, traceably," and nothing about the artifact's polish upgrades the science it reports. The press kit is a faithful messenger; it is not a result.

## Negatives are content (cited as a provenance-flagged snapshot)

The credibility move rests on the program's standing rule, **Section 0 (Negatives are content)**: a partial, a negative, or a "most pieces don't help" decomposition is a *measurement that the design is incomplete*, not an exit and not a failure to hide. The negatives are the credibility.

This is exactly where the fence has to be loudest, because the temptation is to convert the negatives into a single impressive number. The last recorded ledger snapshot is **882 rows = 350 PASS / 0 FAIL / 183 NEGATIVE / 349 PENDING**, and it must be cited **as a provenance-flagged snapshot**, never as a stand-alone re-countable headline. The reason is recorded in the constitution itself: the ledger derives from a deduplicated merge of 615 extracted claims, the snapshot enumerates 882 rows, and the carded body exposes only on the order of 140 distinct rows, so a skeptic counting the published rows **cannot** independently derive 183. Per the calibration-down rule, we headline only the negatives the ledger body actually enumerates (World C is a count baseline, not comprehension and not beats-LLMs; Phase J carries `J.attribution_caveat` where overall NLL worsens; the comprehension-above-retrieval central wall is thrice-NEGATIVE; the metabolism organ helps foraging but building went minus 14 percent; the EDAIT trade is roughly 33 perplexity versus a backprop GPT's roughly 25), and we present the 183 figure only with its provenance flag attached. To feature "183 published negatives" as a bare credibility number, before the count is reconstructable from the published rows, would itself be an overclaim, and it is forbidden.

## The CTA, and the lift we have not earned

The mandated call-to-action is "help us get this independently verified" (a third-party fresh-split challenge), and never "fund the vision." That phrasing is the near-zero-claim-risk posture: it asks the reader to falsify, which is something a true claim survives and a false one does not.

The fence on the *outcome* of this posture is recorded as **TA25 (relationship-first engagement ladder)**, evidence class **B**, and it is unambiguous: **engagement-lift is TARGETED, NOT YET PROVEN (Class B / pending), and must not be raised to Class A.** The ladder (Discover-and-Elevate, Follow-Up, Bridge, then a CTA only at step four when invited), the Deep Tagged Replies, and the own-post defense are a designed mechanism whose origin is a recorded negative: 372 posts produced one comment in 30 days. TA25's falsifier is its registered measurement: over three disciplined weeks, if author-reply rate (25 percent or higher), reply-chain depth (1.8 or higher), and own-post impressions (plus 30 percent in four weeks) fail to move, we revise the playbook, not the volume. Until those targets are met on a held window, the honest copy is that the engagement model is a *bet we are instrumenting*, not a result we are reporting.

### What is NOT claimed in Mv1 - The honesty fence is the pitch

- **Ceiling.** That "we are honest / we publish our negatives" is itself proof that the UNI science is correct, that the program has won its field, or that the engagement strategy works, is NOT shown. The most we claim is: an honesty-and-traceability method exists and is reusable (M15, `method`); it was executed end-to-end to produce one fully ledger-traceable press kit with no server-side LLM (TA3, Class E, test-covered not feature-working); the program publishes its negatives as first-class content and cites the 882 / 183 figure as a provenance-flagged snapshot (Section 0); and the relationship-first engagement lift is targeted but unproven (TA25, Class B / pending).
- **Fences engaged.** Red line 7 (never raise a claim above its source evidence class) governs TA3 at E and TA25 at B. Red line 12, the vocabulary-leak guard, forbids externalizing the active-inference / EFE / free-energy framework names and forbids featuring any internal channel handle in this public copy. Red line 10 (no PII; a link may exist but the handle is never featured) applies to the publishing surface. Red lines 1, 2, 4, and 5 apply by inheritance: the copy this chapter governs never claims AGI, consciousness, created life, or beats-LLMs. The Section 0 provenance fence forbids the bare "183 negatives" headline. The standing program framing applies throughout: developmental SIMULATION, bounded peek, toy world, Class U where not claimed, unrefereed working preprint.
- **Negatives that travel with this claim (cite alongside, never strip).** TA25's "engagement-lift is Class B / pending, do NOT raise to A" (origin: 372 posts to one comment in 30 days) travels with any mention of the engagement ladder. TA3's Class-E falsifiers (untraceable figure / failed one-command reproduction / a server-side content LLM in the path) travel with any "we proved the no-API model" statement. The Section 0 provenance flag (882 rows, only ~140 carded, 183 not re-derivable) travels with the negatives figure every time it appears, alongside the press kit's own front-page negatives (active inference still NEGATIVE on the reader, ~10 to 15 percent behind backprop LLMs by a chosen trade, the audit-driven down-calibrations).
- **Parked / owed.** No sign-to-park is owed by this chapter (M15, TA3, and TA25 are not parked-frontier rows). What is *owed* is empirical: the TA25 engagement-lift targets remain a pending Class-B measurement that can only be moved by observation, never argued up; and the CTA's promise is itself owed a discharge, namely an actual independent third-party fresh-split verification, which has not yet occurred.
- **One-line honest summary a skeptic could not dispute.** We have proven that we can publish a fully ledger-traceable, no-server-LLM press kit and that we lead with our negatives and a "verify us, do not fund us" CTA; we have not proven, and do not here claim, that this honesty makes the underlying science correct or that the engagement strategy works.

Secondarily: if any public copy governed by this chapter is shown carrying the fenced framework vocabulary, featuring a channel handle, presenting the bare "183 negatives" headline without its provenance flag, or running a "fund the vision" CTA, the honesty-fence method (M15) has been violated at its definition.
