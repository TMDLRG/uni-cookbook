# The NATURA wing — nature's regularities, kept sovereign from UNI's build status

The rest of this encyclopedia describes **UNI**: what the program built, at what evidence
class, with the negatives printed. This wing describes **nature**: the regularities that
were measured by other people, in published work, long before UNI existed.

It exists to serve one mandate — that a builder be able to reason about design "down to the
cell level," about "the ratios and frequencies," about "mind body mind.body world," and about
how to "research observe and make new science with falsifiable evidence to always use nature
as the authority of where we will find the next breakthrough" — across rocks, water, air,
stars, DNA, sperm, ants, dinosaurs, whales, bats, humans, and the open question beyond.

That mandate is answerable only if the wing is disciplined about a distinction the rest of the
corpus never had to make: **the difference between a gate UNI held and a measurement somebody
else published.** Both are evidence. They are not the same kind, and they do not license the
same moves.

**The honest program position is untouched by every chapter in this wing, and this wing can
never change it: ~2 of 11+ developmental rungs earned; a developmental active-inference
SIMULATION; a toy world, never a person.** If a NATURA chapter ever appears to move that
number, the chapter is the defect.

## THE SOVEREIGNTY RULE (read this before anything else in the wing)

**A nature citation is NEVER a UNI gate.**

Reading Kleiber's law raises no UNI rung. Citing Douady & Couder does not make any UNI claim
`proven`. A chapter that lets a literature citation imply a UNI capability has committed a
lane-crossing and is defective — regardless of how good the citation is.

This corpus carries **two sovereign evidence vocabularies, and they are never merged:**

| | **The UNI 4-value fence** | **The NATURA 12-value class** |
|---|---|---|
| Describes | **UNI's own build status** | **nature's** observed regularities |
| Values | `proven` · `designed` · `hypothesized` · `not-yet-built` | twelve, in three groups (below) |
| Measured by | UNI's own gates (PASS / FAIL / NEGATIVE / PENDING) | third parties, in published literature |
| Governed by | [`../CLAIM-LEDGER.md`](../CLAIM-LEDGER.md) | [`../NATURE-LEDGER.md`](../NATURE-LEDGER.md) |

**The twelve classes, in three groups** (defined in full, with a worked example of each, in
[NA-00](NA-00-how-to-read-this-wing.md); this is a pointer, not a restatement):

- **Group A — measured** *(the class says how much independent corroboration exists)*:
  `OBSERVED-REPLICATED` · `OBSERVED-SINGLE` · `OBSERVED-CONTESTED`
- **Group B — derived** *(the class says the assumptions are the fence)*:
  `MODELED` · `MODELED-CONTESTED` · `HYPOTHESIZED`
- **Group C — fenced** *(the row carries no usable value; the class says precisely why not —
  these are **not** interchangeable)*: `INADMISSIBLE` · `SUPERSEDED` · `NOT-MEASURED` ·
  `NOT-SOURCED` · `NOT-CONFIRMED` · `NOT-LOCATED`

The two ledgers are cross-referenced **by explicit audited link only, never by merge.** There is
no operation that takes a NATURA row and a UNI row and produces a stronger row of either kind.
**`proven` is reserved for UNI-ledger status and never describes nature's science** — not even
this wing's own best-replicated rows.

**One fence inside Group C is load-bearing and is stated twice on purpose: `NOT-SOURCED` is
never `NOT-MEASURED`.** NOT-MEASURED is an absence **in the literature** — a reportable fact
about the world's knowledge. NOT-SOURCED is an absence **in this corpus's own homework** — a
fact about *us*. Collapsing them would let a research failure masquerade as a discovery about
the frontier of science.

**TRUE and HONEST stay sovereign.** Every one of the twelve classes is a TRUE-side instrument:
falsifiable, reproducible, recalibratable. Lived experience is **never calibrated** — a
chakra-frequency table is INADMISSIBLE as physics and may be recorded as an HONEST/cultural
signal, never as a TRUE/measured one, and never converted between them. The wing's only
HONEST-class row (`CN11-59`, first-person testimony) is deliberately given **no** NATURA class.

**Supremacy.** Where a chapter and the ledger disagree, **the ledger wins and the chapter is
wrong** — the same rule the UNI side already runs.

## Reading order, if you are here to DESIGN something

Canonical in [NA-00](NA-00-how-to-read-this-wing.md#reading-order-if-you-are-here-to-design-something)
and carried here unchanged. The order is deliberate; the reasons are the point.

1. **NA-00** — the vocabulary and the cardinal rule. **Non-skippable.**
2. **NA-03** — the method *before* the content, so you cannot mistake an inspiration for a result.
3. **NA-01** — the authority doctrine **and its counterweight**, together, in that order.
4. **NA-07, then NA-05** — dimensionless numbers before ratios. **Dimensionless groups survive a
   change of scale and most ratios do not.** Reading NA-05 first is how designers end up with
   golden ratios in places nature never put one.
5. **The scale chapter you need** — NA-08 (cell), NA-09 (form), or NA-10 (ladder).
6. **The CN recipe for your target** — and only now.
7. **Back to NA-03** — write the pre-registered metric and the tuned baseline *before* you build.
   A biomimetic design with no baseline is not a design; it is a story.

## The honest residue (stated here, not buried)

Two things these amendments did **not** fix. They are printed in the index because an index that
points only at the clean parts is not an index, it is an advertisement.

1. **The 22 untraced rows.** The `NOT-SOURCED` (20), `NOT-CONFIRMED` (1) and `NOT-LOCATED` (1) rows
   are now *correctly labelled*, which is **not** the same as *fixed*. Twenty-two rows carry values
   this corpus could not fully trace. **Naming a defect precisely is not repairing it.** Each carries
   the falsifier that would close it, and closing them is real work that has not been done.

   `NATURE-LEDGER.md` and K20 — those two files, and nothing else.** **52 rows across 10 of these 23
   chapters went on carrying PRE-amendment class strings** (42 bare/qualified `OBSERVED`, 3
   `NEGATIVE`, 5 `as above`, 2 CN-05 cells not leading with a token) until **AMENDMENT 2026-07-15-C**
   re-classed them in lockstep with the ledger. For one day the extraction and the extracted
   disagreed in the one column the amendment existed to fix — and three of those cells printed
   `NEGATIVE`, a **UNI ledger state**, in the Class column of a NATURA table: the cardinal rule was
   argued correctly in the ledger and violated in the chapters. **The verifier reported PASS anyway,
   because it never opened a chapter.** They are labelled correctly now. What is *not* repaired is
   the reason it happened: nothing in the record disclosed the gap — **it took an outside audit to
   find it.** The instrument is fixed; the habit that produced it is not demonstrated fixed, and one
