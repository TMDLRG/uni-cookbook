## Why this wing exists

The rest of the encyclopedia describes **UNI**: what the program has built, at what evidence class,
with the negatives printed. This wing describes **nature**: the regularities that were measured by
other people, in published work, long before UNI existed.

The wing serves one mandate — that the science consultant be able to reason about design "down to the
cell level," about "the ratios and frequencies," about "mind body mind.body world," and about how to
"research observe and make new science with falsifiable evidence to always use nature as the
authority of where we will find the next breakthrough" — across "dna sperm ants dinosaurs whales bats
rocks water air stars humans" and the open question beyond.

That mandate is answerable only if the wing is disciplined about a distinction the rest of the corpus
never had to make: **the difference between a gate UNI held and a measurement somebody else
published.** Both are evidence. They are not the same kind, they do not license the same moves, and
the whole wing turns on never confusing them.

Per [`../../README.md`](../../README.md), this wing's ledger of record is
[`../NATURE-LEDGER.md`](../NATURE-LEDGER.md). Every row in every NA/CN chapter is written to be
admissible there. Where a chapter and the ledger disagree, **the ledger wins and the chapter is
wrong** — the same supremacy rule the UNI side already runs.

## The cardinal rule

**A nature citation is never a UNI gate.**

Reading Kleiber's law raises no UNI rung. Citing Douady & Couder does not make any UNI claim
`proven`. A chapter that lets a literature citation imply a UNI capability has committed a
lane-crossing and is defective — full stop, regardless of how good the citation is.

This is not a new rule. It is the same fence the corpus already applies to the embodiment substrate
("UNI.OS substrate evidence is **never** general-AIF evidence"), and the same one behind M9's class
authority ordering. The NATURA wing simply gives it a second front. The two ledgers are
cross-referenced **by explicit audited link only, never by merge.** There is no operation that takes
a NATURA row and a UNI row and produces a stronger row of either kind.

The honest program position is unchanged by anything in this wing, and this wing can never change it:
**~2 of 11+ developmental rungs earned.** If a NATURA chapter ever appears to move that number, the
chapter is the defect.

## The classes, with a worked example of each

The UNI fence has four values (`proven` · `designed` · `hypothesized` · `not-yet-built`) and the
UNI ledger has four states (PASS / FAIL / NEGATIVE / PENDING). Neither vocabulary is used in this
wing.

> **STRICKEN 2026-07-15 — the original wording, carried on record:**
> *"This wing uses six classes and only six."*
> **It was false.** 72 of the 919 rows the chapters wrote (7.8%) carried a class string outside the
> six, because the six could not express what those rows had actually observed. See
> [§ Amendment record](#amendment-record-2026-07-15). This wing now uses **twelve classes in three
> groups** — and the count is a measured property of the corpus, not a design target. If a
> thirteenth is needed, that is a finding, and the same amendment procedure applies.

The three groups answer three different questions. **Group A — measured:** somebody put an
instrument on nature; the class says *how much independent corroboration exists*. **Group B —
derived:** the number came out of a model; the class says *the assumptions are the fence*.
**Group C — fenced:** the row carries no usable value, and the class says *precisely why not* — the
ways of being empty are **not interchangeable**, and collapsing them is how a corpus starts lying.

### Group A — measured

**OBSERVED-REPLICATED** — measured, and independently replicated across labs, datasets, or taxa.
Carries a real citation, a falsifier, and the known range.
*Worked example:* the Schumann resonance fundamental, ≈ 7.83 Hz. Predicted by Schumann (1952),
first extracted from background noise by Balser & Wagner (1960), *Nature* 188:638–641, and measured
at ELF stations worldwide since. It is a real Earth–ionosphere cavity resonance. Note what the class
does *not* buy: it licenses the frequency, not any claim attached to the frequency.

**OBSERVED-SINGLE** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 42 rows)* measured, by a method another party could
repeat, but **no independent replication is on record.** One line of evidence: a single study, a
single group, a single report, or a single chain through a review. The class asserts the
measurement and asserts **nothing** about corroboration.
*Why it had to exist — the corpus's own words.* CN-07 stated the reason exactly, and it is the
whole argument for this class: *"OBSERVED-REPLICATED asserts a replication that did not happen, and
OBSERVED-CONTESTED asserts a dispute that does not exist. Both are false, in opposite directions."*
A measured-but-unreplicated result forced into either neighbour is a lie in a known direction;
under the six, 42 rows had no honest home.
*Worked example:* CN09-26 — nitrogen-washout collapse depth in *Tursiops truncatus*, ~70 m
(Ridgway & Howard 1979, *Science* 206(4423):1182–1183). Real measurement, real citation, one group,
never independently repeated.
*Falsifier — the promotion and the demotion, both live:* an independent replication **promotes** it
to OBSERVED-REPLICATED; a failed independent replication **demotes** it to OBSERVED-CONTESTED (or,
if withdrawn, SUPERSEDED). Until one of those happens, the row stays here — and "nobody has tried"
is a fact about the literature, printed as one.

**OBSERVED-CONTESTED** — measured, but the community genuinely disputes the value, the exponent, or
the mechanism. Both positions must be carried and the dispute named. This is a feature, not an
embarrassment.
*Worked example:* the metabolic scaling exponent *b* in BMR ∝ M^*b*. Kleiber (1932) reported ≈ 0.74
(Brody, the same year, ≈ 0.73). Dodds, Rothman & Weitz (2001) re-analysed the classic datasets and
found little evidence for rejecting 2/3 in favour of 3/4. White & Seymour (2003) report
0.686 ± 0.014 (95% CI). The exponent **moves with the analysis choices** — which taxa are excluded,
whether body temperature is normalised, whether the regression unit is the species or the order. That
mobility *is* the dispute, and a chapter that picks one number and drops the other has lied.

### Group B — derived

**MODELED** — derived from a model that fits data. **The model's assumptions are the fence.**
*Worked example:* West, Brown & Enquist (1997) derive *b* = 3/4 exactly, from three assumptions: a
space-filling fractal branching network, terminal units invariant with body size, and minimised
energy dissipation. The derivation is clean. It is also conditional: drop any one assumption and the
3/4 does not follow. Cite it as MODELED and cite the assumptions with it, or do not cite it.

**MODELED-CONTESTED** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 1 row)* derived from a model, **and** the community
genuinely disputes it. Both positions carried, and the model's assumptions still the fence — the row
must survive both tests, not one. This class exists because the original six were treated as
disjoint and are not: MODELED and OBSERVED-CONTESTED overlap in reality, and a row landing in the
overlap was previously forced to drop one of its two true properties.
*Worked example:* CN12-31 — human behavioural throughput, ~10 bit/s (Zheng & Meister 2025, *Neuron*
113(2), a Perspective — a literature synthesis, not a new experiment), directly contested by
Sauerbrei & Pruszynski (2025), *Nat Neurosci*. Modeled **and** disputed; calling it MODELED alone
hides the dispute, calling it OBSERVED-CONTESTED alone claims a measurement nobody made.
*Falsifier:* a measure reconciling cognitive throughput and motor control under one accounting.

**HYPOTHESIZED** — mechanism proposed, not settled. Not claimed.
*Worked example:* the radical-pair mechanism of avian magnetoreception (Ritz et al., 2000; reviewed
in *PNAS*, DOI 10.1073/pnas.0711968106). Radio-frequency disruption experiments are consistent with
it, and behaviour at microtesla fields strengthens it (*Nature Communications*, DOI
10.1038/s41467-019-11655-2). Much of the supporting evidence remains circumstantial, and a competing
magnetite-based hypothesis is live. Birds navigate; *how* is not settled.

### Group C — fenced (the row carries no usable value; the class says why)

**INADMISSIBLE** — a claim that failed observation, or that is unfalsifiable as stated. Carried
**with the receipt of why it failed.** Never asserted, never mocked.
*Worked example:* "432 Hz is nature's true tuning." A4 = 440 Hz is a **convention** — ISO 16:1975
fixes it at 440 ± 0.5 Hz, not a natural constant — and 432 Hz is a different convention. Neither is
more natural, and the claim names no measurand that could distinguish them. Inadmissible as physics
or biology. The person asking is not stupid; the claim is just not shaped like a claim yet.

**SUPERSEDED** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 3 rows)* a value that **was** carried in the literature and
has since been **retracted, withdrawn by its own authors, or contradicted by a calibrated
re-analysis.** Kept as the **receipt for why it must not be cited** — never deleted, because
deleting it is how a superseded number gets re-imported by the next reader who finds it in an old
textbook. Distinct from INADMISSIBLE: an INADMISSIBLE claim was never shaped like a claim; a
SUPERSEDED value was a perfectly good claim that lost.
*Worked example:* CN01-34 — `f_bio,2008` ≈ 2/3 of 4,300 minerals as biologically mediated (Hazen et
al. 2008), **withdrawn by the authors themselves**. Scope cell reads: SUPERSEDED — DO NOT CITE.
*Falsifier:* this class's rows have **already had their falsifier fire.** That is what put them here.
*On the name — read this before "fixing" it.* These three rows were originally written `NEGATIVE`.
This amendment deliberately did **not** register that string, because **`NEGATIVE` is a UNI ledger
state** (PASS / FAIL / NEGATIVE / PENDING) and appears as such in the crosswalk below, where it is
paired against INADMISSIBLE. Registering `NEGATIVE` as a NATURA evidence class would have put one
token in both sovereign vocabularies at once — closing this defect by committing the exact
lane-crossing the cardinal rule exists to forbid. The rows' own cells already said *"SUPERSEDED
FRAMING"*, *"SUPERSEDED — DO NOT CITE"*, and *"contradicted by"*; `SUPERSEDED` is the class that was
always true of them. **The wing's separate, unrelated use of "NEGATIVE" — a biomimetic design that
failed to beat its tuned baseline under M7 — is untouched and stays UNI-side, where it belongs.**

**NOT-MEASURED** — the honest empty face: **nobody has measured it.** Write this instead of inventing
a number.
*Worked example:* the effect size of the Schumann resonance on human physiology at ambient field
strengths. The fields are in the **picotesla** range. This chapter searched and found no
pre-registered, independently replicated dose–response. That is not a refutation — it is an absence,
and the absence gets written down as an absence.

**NOT-SOURCED** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 20 rows)* the chapter **states a value it could not trace
to a source.** Somebody measured it; this corpus could not find who.
**This is NOT the same as NOT-MEASURED, and the difference is the whole point of registering it.**
NOT-MEASURED is an absence **in the literature** — a real, reportable fact about the world's
knowledge. NOT-SOURCED is an absence **in this corpus's own homework** — a fact about *us*, not about
nature. Collapsing the two would let a research failure masquerade as a discovery about the frontier
of science, which is among the worst things this wing could do. **Never merge them.**
*Falsifier:* produce the primary source. The row is then re-classed on its merits (usually
OBSERVED-SINGLE or OBSERVED-REPLICATED) — or, if no source exists, the value is struck.

**NOT-CONFIRMED** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 1 row)* the primary source **is named**, at one remove —
a secondary or tertiary work cites it — but the primary was **not read in this pass.** The provenance
chain is known and unwalked. Distinct from NOT-SOURCED, where no chain was found at all.
*Worked example:* CN10-53 — `f_click,B.trigona` up to 4,500 Hz, via a tertiary source citing Corcoran
et al. 2009; primary not read. *Falsifier:* read Corcoran et al. 2009, *Science* 325:325–7, and
confirm or strike.

**NOT-LOCATED** — *(registered 2026-07-15; 1 row)* a **named source was searched for and does not
appear to exist.** Strongest of the three provenance fences: not "we didn't look" (NOT-SOURCED) and
not "we didn't walk it" (NOT-CONFIRMED), but "we looked, and it wasn't there."
*Worked example:* CN12-40 — doctrine row `D3-08`, cited in the chapter's own commissioning brief; a
grep of `uni-onchip/design/` found no such row. The chapter reports the miss against **its own
brief** rather than quietly satisfying it. *Falsifier:* produce the row, or correct the citation.

> **The provenance fence, in one line.** NOT-MEASURED = *nature has not been asked.*
> NOT-SOURCED = *we cannot say who asked.* NOT-CONFIRMED = *we know who asked and did not read them.*
> NOT-LOCATED = *we checked, and the source named is not there.* Four different failures. Four
> different repairs. One of them is nature's; **three of them are ours.**

### CN12-41 — NONDUM FALSIFICABILIS is a register status, *extra Arborem*

`NONDUM FALSIFICABILIS` ("not yet falsifiable") is **not an evidence class and never was.** It is a
**register status**: the mark of an item held in the QUAESTIONES APERTAE register, **extra Arborem**
(outside the Tree) — *leguntur, non aguntur*: **they are read, not acted upon.**

The distinction is load-bearing. An evidence class says *how well a claim about nature is supported.*
A register status says *this item is not in the ledger at all.* `CN12-41` — `anima in silicio?` /
`silicium ut aqua?` — offers **no falsifier**, and its source cell says why that is correct rather
than defective: **that is exactly why it lives in a register and not in a ledger.** A ledger row

Giving it an evidence class would import it into the Tree by relabelling — admitting through the
vocabulary something that has not passed the admission tests. It stays *extra Arborem*, and it is
**not counted in the twelve.**

## Compound rows (the "disjoint" half of falsifier #2, answered honestly)

Two rows carry **two classes**, because two *parts* of the row have different evidentiary standing:

- `CN05-31` — `ε_MMR`: **MODELED** (the ladder rung) · **OBSERVED-REPLICATED** (the overall band).
- `CN06-30` — **OBSERVED-SINGLE** (the latency) · **MODELED** (its identification with T).

The original design assumed every row lands in **exactly one** class. These two do not, and no
amount of vocabulary fixes that — the *row* has two parts, not the *class system*. Splitting them
into separate rows would be the tidier fix and is **deliberately not done**: the parts are only
meaningful together, and separating them invites a reader to cite the modeled half as if it were the
measured half. That is the merge this wing exists to prevent.

**The convention, registered:** a compound row names **each part's class to its part**, in the class
cell, in the order the parts appear. It is **counted at its leading class** (CN05-31 counts as
MODELED; CN06-30 as OBSERVED-SINGLE) — the count is a convention for arithmetic, and the class cell,
not the count, is authoritative for the row. **Compounds are rare and must stay rare:** two of 919.
A compound is admissible only when the parts genuinely cannot be separated. If they can be, split
them.

## Nature as the authority — stated precisely, and its counterweight

The doctrine is defensible in exactly one form:

> Nature is the authority because it is the only system that has **already run the experiment** — a
> very long parallel search under real physical constraints, in which the failures were deleted.
> Convergent evolution is **evidence of a constraint-optimum.**

And it is honest only with the counterweight, which travels with it **everywhere in this wing**:

> Not every trait is an adaptation (Gould & Lewontin 1979, *Proc. R. Soc. Lond. B* 205(1161):581–598).
> Phylogenetic inertia, drift, developmental constraint, pleiotropy and historical contingency all
> produce features that are not optimal solutions to anything. Nature is full of frozen accidents:
> the vertebrate retina is wired backwards; the recurrent laryngeal nerve takes its detour.

Therefore: **"nature does it this way" is a hypothesis generator, never a proof.** A biomimetic
design must still beat a **tuned conventional baseline** on a **pre-registered metric**, or it is
recorded NEGATIVE — the wing inherits M7 (contains-baseline + load-bearing discriminator) unchanged.
That paragraph is the entire difference between this wing and cargo-cult biomimicry.

The wing's own poster child polices this. Spiral phyllotaxis really does cluster near the golden
angle, and Douady & Couder (1992) reproduced it in a **physical ferrofluid-droplet experiment** from
simple repulsion dynamics — mechanism, not mysticism. It is earned. And in the largest sunflower
study with explicit, checkable inclusion criteria, Swinton & Ochu (2016) found **18% of scored counts
were non-Fibonacci** — roughly one in five, in the organism the claim is named after. The regularity
is real. The universality is not. Both facts are printed here, together, and the second one is the
reason to trust the first.

## The honesty rail

- **RES IPSAE, NON SIMULACRA.** No fabricated numbers. Every value carries a real citation, or it is
  written `NOT-MEASURED`. **There is no third state.** A plausible-sounding number with no source is
  the worst defect available in this wing. If you half-remember a value, search for it; if you cannot
  source it, write NOT-MEASURED.
- Every numeric claim carries **value + units + scope + class + source + falsifier**. A number
  without units or scope is a defect.
- **SIGNUM SIGNUM MANET.** The signal and the commentary about the signal travel separately: the
  measured value goes in the table, the interpretation goes in the prose. Never smuggle an
  interpretation into a value. (White & Seymour title their paper "…proportional to body mass^2/3";
  the *title* is commentary, the *CI* is the signal. Read the CI.)
- **Banned in author voice** (quote-with-attribution only): *verified, secure, guaranteed, certified,
  self-aware, conscious, AGI, intelligent*. **`proven` is reserved for UNI-ledger status and never
  describes nature's science.** Use observed / measured / replicated / modeled / BOUNDED / PENDING /
  CONTESTED.
- **TRUE** signals (falsifiable, reproducible, recalibratable) and **HONEST** signals (lived
  experience, never calibrated) stay sovereign. A chakra-frequency table is INADMISSIBLE as physics
  and may be recorded as an HONEST/cultural signal — never as a TRUE/measured one, and never
  converted from one to the other.
- **"Full human" and "the next evolution beyond human" are permanent open questions** —
  QUAESTIO-APERTA. Never a target, never a milestone, never a deliverable.
- Observed / claimed / inferred / unknown stay visibly distinct.
