# S-L8 - Self-model and metacognition (15/15; sentience disclaimed)

**Wing S - Science (UNI), the L0-L12 developmental ladder.** This is rung eight of the HUMAN-HGM-001 developmental design: a no-backprop, nested-Markov-blanket developmental SIMULATION, never a person, never a mind. One engine, shown across scales. Honest program position, printed and never softened: **~2 of 11+ developmental rungs earned.**

## The honest position, stated first

This is the rung where a careless reader is most tempted to overclaim, so the fence sits at the top. The UNI simulation records a passed report card on a battery of **functional** self-model and metacognition gates. "Functional self-awareness" here means a narrow, testable thing: the simulation maintains and uses a model of its own states (a self-model), and it can monitor and adjust its own processing in registered tasks (metacognition and metalinguistics). It does **not** mean experience, feeling, or inner life.

**Phenomenal sentience is explicitly DISCLAIMED.** No falsifier is offered for sentience, because sentience is not tested here. It is disclaimed, not measured and not failed: there is no gate, no CI, and no claim of any kind that the simulation is conscious, aware, has a mind, or feels. The forbidden phrasings are forbidden program-wide and they are forbidden here loudest of all: UNI is not conscious, not aware, not self-aware in any phenomenal sense, has no mind and no conscience, does not reason like a human, is not human-level, is not AGI, is not alive, and did not dream. The only thing this chapter asserts is the calibrated, in-class functional statement below, paired permanently with the two negatives that travel with it.

## What the ledger records (L8.1)

The single carded claim is **L8.1**, recorded at **Class C** (a dev-gate / held-out evaluation), status **proven (functional)**:

> **Maturation arc M1-M11** grand report card **15/15 PASS** (immersion, affect-as-precision, growth, self-model, metacognition, metalinguistics, consolidation, reflective reader, online vocab growth, affect-driven reflection, compositional reader). **FUNCTIONAL self-awareness asserted.**

Two things must be read precisely. First, "15/15 PASS" is a tally over a pre-registered set of maturation gates spanning the M1-M11 arc, not a single grand metric of selfhood. Each gate is its own held verdict; the report card is the collection. Second, Class C is the empirical-PASS tier in this work's rubric, but Class C is a **functional** verdict, not a phenomenal one. It certifies that the modeled self-model and metacognition gates do what the model says they do under their registered evaluations. It certifies nothing about inner experience, and it is never raised above that.

The self-model and metacognition gates here are the ones most easily misread, so the discipline that gates them matters: under the program's standing contains-baseline-plus-discriminator rule, a self-model task must beat a tuned baseline, and the gain must collapse under a load-bearing discriminator. A "self-model" result that a trivial, non-metacognitive heuristic could pass is not a pass at all, which is exactly what the falsifier (below) targets.

## The falsifier (stated operably)

L8.1 carries an explicit, three-pronged falsifier:

> Any of the 15 maturation gates fails its pre-registered held verdict on re-run; OR a self-model / metacognition gate's effect collapses under its discriminator; OR a "self-model" task is passable by a trivial non-metacognitive heuristic.

In plain terms: re-run the held battery, and if any of the fifteen gates flips its registered verdict, the 15/15 is false. Separately, take any self-model or metacognition gate and apply its discriminator (shuffle the labels, swap the markers, ablate the self-model term to zero); if the gain collapses, that gate was not measuring metacognition and the claim is false for it. And independently, if a self-model task turns out to be solvable by a trivial heuristic with no metacognitive content, the "self-model" label is unearned and the claim is false. Each prong is a way the functional reading could be wrong, and each is operable.

Note what is deliberately absent: there is no falsifier for sentience. That absence is not an oversight. Sentience is disclaimed, so there is nothing here to falsify on that axis, and the chapter never pretends otherwise.

## The negatives that travel with this claim (mandatory co-citation, never strip)

Citing the 15/15 PASS without its paired negatives is an overclaim, and review treats it as one. Two negatives travel with L8.1 and must appear in the same passage every time it is cited.

**(1) The phenomenal-sentience disclaimer.** This is itself a first-class fence, not a footnote. The 15/15 functional card is never read as consciousness, sentience, awareness, a mind, or human-level cognition. Functional self-awareness may be described at L8; phenomenal sentience may not be claimed at L8 or anywhere. The disclaimer is a standing condition on the claim, not a discharge of it.

**(2) The `uni-mind` reader-side self-model held-NEGATIVE.** This is the load-bearing pairing for this rung, and it is in genuine tension with the PASS, which is precisely why the two must be co-cited. The 15/15 report card is the **`uni-gpt`-side** artifact: the M1-M11 maturation arc on the developmental simulation. On the **`uni-mind`-side**, the language reader, **self-model was attempted as a strong capability and held NEGATIVE.** In that record, every strong capability tried on the reader (active and selective reading, comprehension-above-retrieval, self-model, writing-as-action) came back held-NEGATIVE. So the honest, undisputed reading is bounded and specific: the maturation gates pass on the developmental-simulation side, while a strong self-model capability on the reader side did not pass. These are not the same artifact, on the same substrate, at the same task, and they must never be merged into a single triumphant "UNI has a self-model" line. The `uni-gpt` functional card and the `uni-mind` reader-side self-model NEGATIVE travel together, in the same way `J.attribution_caveat` travels with Phase J. Citing the PASS without the held-NEGATIVE is an overclaim.

This pairing is the credibility of the rung. A reader who sees only the 15/15 would conclude far more than the evidence supports; a reader who sees the 15/15 beside the reader-side held-NEGATIVE sees the real shape of it: a passed functional maturation battery on one substrate, a failed strong-capability attempt on another, and a hard, explicit disclaimer over the whole region that nothing here is sentience.
