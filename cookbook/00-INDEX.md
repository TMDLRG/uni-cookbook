## The fence label on every recipe

Each recipe closes with one honest fence drawn from a four-value vocabulary:

- **proven** — a held, sealed, UNI-signed PASS exists (Class A/C), falsifier still live.
- **designed** — a typed spec / signed-in-principle design exists; the build is partial or unverified.
- **hypothesized** — a stated mechanism or law with no sealed gate yet (Class U, not claimed).
- **not-yet-built** — no engine, no run, no gate; north-star, hard-fenced.

Every SIGNED consult design (the L2 `build_epistemic_frontier` organ, the L5 Design #3 servo
bridge, L9-G1, L11-R1, the L12 bound paragraph, the C10 Dirichlet-first port) is folded in as
**designed / not-run: it raises nothing and the rung's status is unchanged.**
